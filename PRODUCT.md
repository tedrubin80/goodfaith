# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Indie record label operators who **self-host** Good Faith (Docker Compose or their own infra). They land on the local install homepage after `docker compose up` — not a SaaS marketing funnel. Success = they understand the instance is running, know how to seed a label and sign in, and trust that their royalty data stays under their control.

## Product Purpose

Good Faith Record Management is **open-source, self-hosted** record label management software: distributor-agnostic royalties, splits, rights, and role-based portals. There is **no SaaS subscription billing** (no Stripe/PayPal plans). Artist royalty payouts use mark-paid + ACH CSV export. The homepage at the portal root is an install welcome surface, not a waitlist or pricing page.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, transparent, expert-calm. Reads like serious financial/ops tooling a label finance person would trust with statements — not a flashy consumer music app. Comfortable rather than hyped.

## Anti-references

- SaaS landing pages with pricing tiers, waitlists, and “book a demo” gates.
- Generic cream/gradient SaaS templates and DistroKid-style DIY hype.
- Enterprise sales-gated tools with no public docs.

## Design Principles

1. **Trust is shown** — health status, clear first-run steps, data export, RBAC.
2. **Self-host first** — copy assumes the visitor already installed the stack.
3. **No subscription CTAs** — never pitch plans, trials, or payment processors for access.
4. **Speak the market’s language** — ISRC/UPC, distributors, splits, recoupment.
5. **Professional, not sterile** — specific to music-label ops.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA: contrast, keyboard nav, `prefers-reduced-motion` for animations.
